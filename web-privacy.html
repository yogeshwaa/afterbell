Your timetable stays on your device.

Afterbell Web reads your chosen ICS file locally to display classes, rooms, teachers and countdowns. It saves your timetable and optional background as encrypted data in this browser using a locally stored encryption key. Preferences are also stored locally. Your unlocked browser can read the data; this is not a password-protected vault.

Your timetable and media are never uploaded. There are no app accounts, analytics, ads or cloud sync. Each browser and device has its own data, separate from the Chrome extension. Your original files remain untouched.

The app downloads its code, fonts, logos and help images from GitHub Pages. GitHub processes hosting requests under its privacy statement. The app caches these public files for offline use after the initial visit. Your timetable is kept separately in local browser storage.

Use Settings → Privacy → Delete all local data to remove your saved timetable, media, preferences and encryption key. Clearing this site's browser data also removes its offline files. Private browsing, storage limits or browser cleanup can remove saved data, so keep your original ICS export for re-importing.

No background reminders are provided by the web version. Keep Afterbell open for its live countdown. You can add it to your home screen through your browser's menu or Share options.
